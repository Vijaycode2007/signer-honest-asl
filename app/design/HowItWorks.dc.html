How it works

This app reads fingerspelling, one letter at a time.

Photograph a hand shape and it guesses which of 29 signs you made: the letters A to Z, plus space, delete, and nothing. It cannot read whole-word signs, facial grammar, or movement.

How often is it right?

It depends entirely on who is signing - and most apps don't tell you that.

Why it sometimes refuses

A wrong letter announced confidently is worse than no letter at all. When the model's own confidence falls below {{threshText}}, it says nothing rather than guess. That cut-off isn't a guess either - it was measured, and at that setting it answers about nine photos in ten.
